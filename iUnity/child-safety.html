SAFETY & TRUST

Child Safety Standards

EFFECTIVE DATE: JULY 24, 2026

At iUnity, we are committed to providing a safe environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) or any content or behavior that endangers children.

shield

Our Commitment

iUnity is designed to foster positive community engagement while maintaining a safe platform for everyone.

We strictly prohibit:

  • block Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • block Child sexual exploitation or abuse
  • block Grooming or predatory behavior
  • block Sexualization or exploitation of minors
  • block Any content that violates applicable child protection laws
report

Reporting Safety Concerns

If you become aware of content or behavior that may violate these standards, please report it immediately.

Email: admin@jointoda.com

We review all reports promptly and take appropriate action, which may include:

  • check_circle Removing violating content
  • check_circle Suspending or permanently banning accounts
  • check_circle Cooperating with law enforcement where required by applicable laws
gavel

Compliance

iUnity complies with applicable child safety laws and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

We continuously improve our moderation systems, reporting processes, and platform safety measures to help protect children and maintain a secure community for all users.
